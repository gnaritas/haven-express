Privacy

A simple site that watches nobody.

This site sets no cookies, loads no advertising or analytics scripts, and follows you nowhere. Here is everything it does with information:

The contact form

What you type — your name, and any phone number or email you choose to add, plus your message — is sent to the shop’s own inbox so the people behind the counter can answer. It isn’t sold, shared, or added to mailing lists by this site.

The map embed

The directions map on this site loads map tiles from Google, which may see your IP address when the map paints. Outside that, no third party loads anything on these pages.

Nothing else

No pixels, no ad networks, no analytics dashboards quietly counting your visits. Links out to Google Maps, Facebook, or Instagram carry the usual privacy practices of those services once you click them — nothing is shared on the way out.
