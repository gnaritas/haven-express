The room

A storefront where the truck’s crowd can finally sit down.

For years the brand’s own posts were a running map of where the truck was parked tonight. Now they show one address — 1217 Leavenworth St — and the same plates, served to a room instead of a window line.

Food and drinks are both on the shop’s menu board for its feed, and the weeknight doors close at nine while the weekend ones hold till half past eleven — long enough that dinner can turn into a second round without anyone moving venues.
