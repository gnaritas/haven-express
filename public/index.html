The story

From a truck window to a front door.

Haven Express spent years on Omaha’s streets, building a following in the thousands — nearly 20,000 fans across the brand’s own social pages. People tracked the truck, saved their plates, and asked the same question every time: where is it parked for good?

In October 2026, it answered. The brand’s first brick-and-mortar opened on Leavenworth Street, in the Old Market — keeping the name locals already used, and adding what a truck can’t carry: a menu you can sit down to, drinks to go with it, and weekend hours that run till 11:30.

Opening confirmed by Grow Omaha’s new-restaurants list (Oct 16, 2026) and the shop’s own posts.
